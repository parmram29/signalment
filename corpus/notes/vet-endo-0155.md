## Insulin dysregulation

EMS encompasses insulin resistance, regional adiposity, and increased laminitis risk. Adipose tissue releases inflammatory mediators that interfere with insulin signaling. Certain breeds (Pony, Morgan, Paso Fino) show genetic predisposition to insulin resistance.

## Regional adiposity patterns

Cresty neck, tailhead fat pads, and prepuce fat accumulation characterize EMS. These deposits release inflammatory cytokines and resist leptin signaling. Adiponectin levels decrease, further impairing insulin sensitivity.

## Laminitis pathogenesis

Hyperinsulinemia causes laminar separation through unknown mechanisms, possibly involving hyperinsulinemic hyperleptinemia. Dietary carbohydrates trigger episodes in susceptible individuals. Prevention focuses on dietary restriction and weight management.
