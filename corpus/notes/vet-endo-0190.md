## Atypical PPID presentation

Some horses with pituitary pars intermedia dysfunction present without the classic signs of hypertrichosis or with minimal hirsutism. These cases may show primarily laminitis, muscle wasting, or recurrent infections without obvious coat changes. Recognition requires high index of suspicion and appropriate diagnostic testing.

## Diagnostic challenges

Atypical cases may have normal resting ACTH concentrations but abnormal response to TRH stimulation. Seasonal variations in hormone concentrations must be considered. Differentiating from other causes of laminitis or muscle wasting requires systematic approach and appropriate testing.

## Management considerations

Treatment with pergolide remains the standard approach, though dosing may need adjustment based on individual response. Monitoring involves clinical signs and potentially serial hormone measurements. Prognosis varies depending on severity of clinical signs and owner compliance with long-term management.
