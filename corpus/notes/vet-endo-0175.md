## Estrous cycle hormonal patterns

The canine estrous cycle spans approximately 6-12 months and includes proestrus, estrus, diestrus, and anestrus phases. Estradiol rises during proestrus and early estrus, triggering the LH surge that causes ovulation. Progesterone rises post-ovulation and remains elevated during diestrus regardless of pregnancy status.

## LH surge and ovulation timing

The preovulatory LH surge occurs during late proestrus/early estrus, typically lasting 48-72 hours. Ovulation occurs 1-2 days post-LH surge. Timing of ovulation can be determined by measuring LH or progesterone rise, with ovulation occurring when progesterone exceeds 5-7 ng/mL.

## Diestrus physiology

Corpora lutea persist and secrete progesterone for ~60-90 days regardless of pregnancy status. This extended luteal phase maintains endometrial preparation and can complicate interpretation of some diagnostic tests. Prolactin elevation supports mammary development and maternal behavior during late diestrus.
