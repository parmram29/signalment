## Melanotrope hyperplasia

PPID results from dopaminergic neuron degeneration in the hypothalamus, reducing dopamine inhibition of pars intermedia melanotropes. This leads to hyperplasia and eventual adenoma formation. Unlike human or canine pituitary disease, the pars intermedia is primarily affected rather than pars distalis.

## Hormonal excess consequences

Overproduction of pro-opiomelanocortin (POMC) derivatives including ACTH, alpha-MSH, and beta-endorphin occurs. Elevated ACTH drives hypercortisolemia, though not to the degree seen in canine PDH. Clinical signs include hirsutism, muscle wasting, and recurrent infections.

## Diagnostic approach

Basal ACTH measurement is most commonly used but subject to seasonal variation (highest in fall). TRH stimulation testing may improve sensitivity. Measurement of alpha-MSH or other POMC derivatives remains research applications. Endoscopic examination of pars intermedia is rarely performed.
