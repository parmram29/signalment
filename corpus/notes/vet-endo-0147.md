## Primary lymphocytic thyroiditis

Immune-mediated destruction of thyroid parenchyma accounts for ~50% of cases. Anti-thyroglobulin antibodies serve as markers but do not directly cause tissue damage. Genetic predisposition affects certain breeds (Golden Retrievers, Doberman Pinschers, Cocker Spaniels).

## Idiopathic thyroid atrophy

Non-inflammatory thyroid parenchymal loss accounts for ~30% of cases. Follicular cell destruction leads to glandular atrophy and fibrosis. The distinction from lymphocytic thyroiditis may be difficult histologically.

## Laboratory interpretation challenges

Total T4 concentrations vary with illness, medications, and breed. Free T4 by equilibrium dialysis is preferred for screening. Low TSH supports the diagnosis, but normal TSH does not exclude hypothyroidism. TSH response to TRH stimulation historically used but rarely performed now.
