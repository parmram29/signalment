## Somatotroph adenomas

GH-secreting pituitary adenomas cause soft tissue proliferation, organomegaly, and insulin resistance. The tumors are typically macroadenomas (>1cm) at diagnosis due to delayed recognition of subtle signs. Local mass effects may cause neurologic deficits.

## Insulin resistance mechanism

Growth hormone acts as an anti-insulin hormone, promoting lipolysis and gluconeogenesis. Concurrent IGF-1 elevation further contributes to insulin resistance. Up to 25% of diabetic cats have underlying acromegaly, requiring higher insulin doses for regulation.

## Clinical recognition

Subtle features include broad facial features, enlarged tongue, interdigital skin tags, and organomegaly on imaging. Cardiomyopathy (typically concentric hypertrophy) develops in most cases. Early recognition requires high index of suspicion in poorly regulated diabetics.
