## Stress-induced insulin resistance

Swine show significant insulin resistance in response to acute and chronic stressors including transport, handling, temperature extremes, and social stress. Cortisol and catecholamines contribute to glucose elevation and insulin resistance. This has important implications for meat quality and animal welfare.

## Dietary influences on glucose homeostasis

High-energy diets rich in simple carbohydrates can lead to glucose intolerance and insulin resistance in swine. Fiber content affects glucose absorption and insulin response. Feeding patterns (meal-fed vs. ad libitum) influence insulin sensitivity and glucose homeostasis.

## Production implications

Insulin resistance affects growth efficiency and carcass composition. Animals with altered glucose metabolism may show inconsistent growth rates and altered fat deposition. Management strategies focus on minimizing stress and optimizing dietary formulation to maintain glucose homeostasis.
