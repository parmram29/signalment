## Delta cell tumor characteristics

Somatostatinomas arise from pancreatic delta cells producing excessive somatostatin, which inhibits multiple hormones including insulin, glucagon, gastrin, and growth hormone. These tumors are typically malignant with metastatic potential. Clinical signs reflect hormonal deficiencies caused by somatostatin's inhibitory effects.

## Clinical presentation

Diabetes mellitus develops due to somatostatin-mediated insulin suppression. Steatorrhea and malabsorption result from decreased pancreatic enzyme secretion and gastric acid production. Cholelithiasis occurs due to decreased gallbladder contractility from CCK inhibition.

## Diagnostic challenges

Measuring somatostatin is difficult due to its short half-life and rapid degradation. Diagnosis relies on clinical presentation, ruling out other causes of diabetes and maldigestion, and imaging studies revealing pancreatic masses. Histopathology confirms delta cell origin with somatostatin immunostaining.
