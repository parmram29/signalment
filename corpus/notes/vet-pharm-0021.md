## Phase II conjugation varies by species

Phase II metabolism conjugates a drug or its phase I metabolite to a polar
group, increasing water solubility for excretion. The dominant pathways —
glucuronidation, sulfation, acetylation, glutathione conjugation — are not
equally available across species.

Cats have limited glucuronidation capacity for many substrates. Dogs are poor
acetylators. Pigs conjugate sulfate inefficiently. A drug cleared mainly by a
pathway a species lacks will show a longer half-life and a narrower margin of
safety in that species.

## Consequence for extrapolation

Dose and interval cannot be carried across species by bodyweight scaling alone.
This note is tagged `multi` because it applies to every species and should
survive a species filter rather than be excluded by it.
