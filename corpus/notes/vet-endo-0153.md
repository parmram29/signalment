## Central diabetes insipidus

Failure of AVP (arginine vasopressin) synthesis or release from the posterior pituitary causes inability to concentrate urine. Causes include trauma, neoplasia (pituitary adenomas, craniopharyngiomas), infiltrative diseases (histiocytic diseases, fungal infections), or idiopathic degeneration.

## Nephrogenic diabetes insipidus

Renal tubule resistance to ADH results from congenital defects in V2 receptors or aquaporin channels, or acquired causes including hypercalcemia, hypokalemia, or nephrotoxic drugs (aminoglycosides, amphotericin B).

## Water deprivation testing

Differentiates DI from other causes of PU/PD. Vasopressin response testing distinguishes central from nephrogenic forms. Plasma/urine osmolality ratios and copeptin levels provide additional diagnostic information.
