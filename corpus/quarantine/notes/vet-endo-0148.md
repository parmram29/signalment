## Beta-cell hyperplasia progression

Ferret insulinomas typically arise from diffuse beta-cell hyperplasia that progresses to multiple focal nodules and eventually discrete tumors. The high carbohydrate diet fed to pet ferrets may contribute through chronic insulin stimulation, though the exact etiology remains unclear.

## Clinical presentation

Hypoglycemic episodes produce neuroglycopenic signs: weakness, lethargy, ptyalism, pawing at mouth, seizures. Signs are often episodic and may be precipitated by fasting. Differentiating from idiopathic epilepsy requires concurrent glucose measurement during episodes.

## Medical management

Diazoxide antagonizes insulin release by opening KATP channels. Octreotide, a somatostatin analog, provides alternative therapy. Dietary management with frequent, high-protein meals helps minimize hypoglycemic episodes. Partial pancreatectomy may provide temporary relief but recurrence is common.
