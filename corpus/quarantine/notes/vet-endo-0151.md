## Post-surgical etiology

Iatrogenic hypoparathyroidism most commonly follows thyroidectomy or removal of cervical masses. The parathyroid glands have shared blood supply with thyroid tissue, making surgical devascularization common. Transient hypocalcemia occurs in up to 25% of thyroidectomies.

## Calcium homeostasis disruption

PTH deficiency impairs renal calcium resorption and bone mobilization. Rapid onset hypocalcemia causes membrane instability, leading to hyperexcitability of peripheral nerves. The ionized calcium fraction is physiologically active and determines clinical signs.

## Emergency treatment protocol

IV calcium gluconate (10%) administered slowly with cardiac monitoring. Magnesium sulfate if concurrent hypomagnesemia. Long-term management includes calcitriol and oral calcium supplementation. Monitoring for signs of oversupplementation (hypercalcemia) is essential.
