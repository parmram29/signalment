## Aquaporin-2 dysfunction in cats

Nephrogenic diabetes insipidus in cats may result from congenital defects in aquaporin-2 water channels or acquired defects due to tubular damage. AQP2 is essential for collecting duct water permeability and responds to ADH stimulation. Genetic mutations in AQP2 genes cause hereditary nephrogenic DI in some cats.

## Acquired causes and pathophysiology

Tubulointerstitial nephritis, pyelonephritis, or exposure to nephrotoxic drugs can damage collecting ducts and impair AQP2 function. Hypercalcemia and hypokalemia affect AQP2 trafficking and expression. The kidneys lose the ability to concentrate urine despite adequate ADH presence.

## Management strategies

Treatment focuses on addressing underlying causes when possible. Thiazide diuretics paradoxically reduce urine volume by inducing mild volume depletion and enhancing proximal tubule reabsorption. Dietary modifications to increase protein and salt intake may help stimulate thirst and fluid intake to prevent dehydration.
