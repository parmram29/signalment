## Normal HPA axis function

The hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal axis coordinates the response to stress through CRH, ACTH, and cortisol. Cats show unique aspects of HPA function including different diurnal rhythms and sensitivity to environmental stressors. Understanding normal function is crucial for interpreting dysfunction.

## Altered stress responses

Chronic stress, illness, or medication may alter HPA axis sensitivity and responsiveness. Cats with chronic diseases often show blunted stress responses. Environmental enrichment and stress reduction may help restore normal HPA function in some cases.

## Clinical implications

Abnormal HPA axis function affects recovery from illness and response to treatment. Cats with altered stress responses may have prolonged recovery times or difficulty adapting to environmental changes. Monitoring and managing stress is important for patient outcomes.
