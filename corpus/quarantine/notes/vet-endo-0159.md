## Physiological stress response

Cattle mount robust cortisol responses to transport, handling, thermal stress, parturition, and disease. The hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal axis activates rapidly, with cortisol peaking within 30-60 minutes of stressor onset. Chronic stress leads to sustained hypercortisolemia.

## Impact on production parameters

Elevated cortisol suppresses growth hormone and prolactin, reducing milk yield and growth rates. Gluconeogenesis increases glucose availability but may compromise immune function. Reproductive hormones are suppressed, affecting fertility and conception rates.

## Management implications

Handling stress should be minimized around breeding, calving, and milking. Environmental enrichment and gentle handling techniques reduce stress responses. Monitoring cortisol levels in hair or milk provides chronic stress assessment.
