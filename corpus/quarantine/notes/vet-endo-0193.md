## Clinical presentation in rabbits

Hyperthyroidism is rare in rabbits but has been documented, particularly in older animals. Signs may include weight loss despite normal or increased appetite, increased activity, tachycardia, and heat intolerance. Differentiating from other causes of weight loss requires careful diagnostic workup.

## Diagnostic challenges

Normal reference ranges for rabbit thyroid hormones have limited availability. Sample collection can be stressful, potentially affecting results. Differentiating true hyperthyroidism from stress-induced changes or concurrent illness requires careful clinical correlation and possibly serial measurements.

## Management approach

Treatment options for hyperthyroid rabbits are limited and often involve careful monitoring and supportive care. Methimazole may be used with careful monitoring for side effects. Prognosis varies depending on the underlying cause and overall health status of the rabbit.
