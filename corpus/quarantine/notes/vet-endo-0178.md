## Thyroid dysgenesis and dyshormonogenesis

Congenital hypothyroidism results from thyroid gland agenesis (dysgenesis) or defective hormone synthesis (dyshormonogenesis). The condition affects neurodevelopment and skeletal maturation during critical growth periods. Breeds including Golden Retrievers and Fox Terriers show increased prevalence.

## Clinical manifestations

Affected puppies show growth retardation, delayed bone maturation, and intellectual disability. Retarded closure of fontanelles and epiphyseal plates occurs. Mental dullness, thickened facial features, and dwarfism characterize the condition. Coat abnormalities include alopecia and thickened skin.

## Therapeutic intervention

Early levothyroxine supplementation may partially reverse some developmental abnormalities. Treatment initiated after critical developmental windows may result in permanent deficits. Monitoring growth parameters and adjusting dosages based on clinical signs and hormone concentrations is essential.
