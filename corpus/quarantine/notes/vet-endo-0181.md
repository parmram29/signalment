## Developmental pituitary anomalies

Combined pituitary hormone deficiency (CPHD) results from abnormal pituitary development during embryogenesis. The condition affects multiple hormone axes simultaneously, including GH, TSH, ACTH, LH, FSH, and prolactin. German Shepherds and Carnelian Bear Dogs show inherited forms.

## Clinical presentation

Affected puppies exhibit proportional dwarfism with delayed skeletal maturation and delayed closure of growth plates. Hypothyroidism and hypoadrenocorticism may be present. Cutaneous abnormalities include retention of puppy coat, hyperpigmentation, and delayed tooth eruption. Intelligence may be affected.

## Management approach

Treatment involves lifelong hormone replacement including growth hormone, thyroid hormone, and glucocorticoids. Monitoring involves growth parameters, skeletal development, and metabolic status. Prognosis depends on owner compliance and early recognition of the condition.
