## Clinical presentation in birds

Avian hypothyroidism manifests as hyperkeratosis of the cere and feet, poor plumage quality, and delayed molting. Behavioral changes including decreased activity and vocalization may occur. Reproductive dysfunction is common in breeding birds. Differentiation from other causes of poor feather quality requires systematic evaluation.

## Diagnostic challenges

Reference ranges for avian thyroid hormones are limited and vary significantly between species. Sample timing relative to molt cycle affects interpretation. Stress and illness alter thyroid hormone concentrations through non-thyroidal illness syndrome. Baseline T4 and T3 concentrations require species-specific interpretation.

## Treatment considerations

Levothyroxine dosing in birds requires careful consideration of species-specific metabolism and protein binding. Monitoring involves clinical signs and periodic laboratory assessment. Dietary factors including goitrogenic compounds affect thyroid function and treatment efficacy.
