## Molecular mechanisms

Insulin resistance in horses involves impaired insulin signaling through the PI3K-AKT pathway. Factors include inflammation, oxidative stress, and altered adipokine profiles. Adipose tissue releases inflammatory mediators that interfere with insulin receptor function.

## Assessment methods

Dynamic insulin sensitivity tests provide more accurate assessment than static measures. Frequently sampled IV glucose tolerance test (FSIGTT) with minimal model analysis is considered the gold standard. Oral sugar tests using standardized solutions offer practical field alternatives.

## Clinical implications

Insulin-resistant horses have increased laminitis risk, even without overt EMS. Monitoring involves serial glucose and insulin measurements, potentially with dynamic testing. Management focuses on diet modification, exercise, and weight reduction when applicable.
