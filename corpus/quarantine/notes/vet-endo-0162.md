## Glycated protein formation

Fructosamine represents glycated serum proteins (primarily albumin) formed through non-enzymatic glycation over approximately 2-3 weeks. Glycated hemoglobin (HbA1c) reflects glucose exposure over the erythrocyte lifespan (~60 days in cats). Both provide retrospective glucose control assessment.

## Interpretation challenges

Stress hyperglycemia may elevate blood glucose without corresponding increases in glycated proteins. Conditions affecting protein metabolism, albumin turnover, or erythrocyte lifespan alter test interpretation. Hyperthyroidism and corticosteroid therapy affect both glucose homeostasis and protein metabolism.

## Clinical application

Fructosamine is preferred for monitoring therapy response due to shorter integration period. Values >350 μmol/L suggest inadequate control. HbA1c provides longer-term perspective but requires species-specific reference ranges. Serial measurements track trends more reliably than single values.
