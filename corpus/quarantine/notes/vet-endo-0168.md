## Causes of adrenal insufficiency

Primary adrenal insufficiency in reptiles may result from infectious processes (fungal, bacterial), neoplastic infiltration, or autoimmune destruction. Secondary causes include pituitary dysfunction or exogenous corticosteroid administration with subsequent suppression. Chronic stress may lead to adrenal exhaustion.

## Clinical presentation

Reptiles with adrenal insufficiency may present with anorexia, weight loss, and decreased activity. Unlike mammals, reptiles lack obvious signs of hypotension or electrolyte abnormalities. Immunosuppression may predispose to concurrent infections. Breeding animals may show reproductive dysfunction.

## Diagnostic approach

Baseline corticosterone concentrations have limited utility due to significant diurnal and seasonal variation. ACTH stimulation testing is the preferred diagnostic method. Sample timing relative to light-dark cycles and seasonal periods is crucial for interpretation.
