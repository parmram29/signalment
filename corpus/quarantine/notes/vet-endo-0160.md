## Clinical presentation in rabbits

Rabbit hypothyroidism presents subtly with weight gain, decreased activity, and coarse haircoat. Unlike dogs, myxedema is rarely observed. Changes in molting patterns and cold intolerance may be noted. Differentiating from other causes of lethargy and weight gain requires careful evaluation.

## Diagnostic limitations

Baseline T4 and T3 concentrations are challenging to interpret due to limited reference ranges and physiological variation. TSH measurement is not routinely available for rabbits. Response to levothyroxine trial therapy may support diagnosis when laboratory confirmation is elusive.

## Treatment considerations

Levothyroxine dosing in rabbits requires careful titration due to sensitivity to medication. Monitoring for signs of hyperthyroidism is essential during therapy. Dietary considerations include ensuring adequate iodine intake, though deficiency is rare in commercial rabbit diets.
