# Quarantine

Content that is **not loaded** by the retrieval system. `load_corpus` reads
`corpus/notes/` only, so nothing here reaches an index, a gold set, or a metric.

Nothing here is deleted. It is parked because it cannot currently make the claim
its front-matter made.

## What is in here

`notes/` — 51 notes generated by an AI assistant in commit `1e8df26`, plus two
(`*.cline.md`) that overwrote curated examples. They are fluent and mostly
plausible. That is the problem: they were marked `confidence: verified` with
specific clinical figures and named citations that no human had checked.

An example of what makes this unusable as-is:

```yaml
confidence: verified
provenance: literature-review     # not a value the schema accepts
authored: 2024-06-17              # backdated; nobody authored it then
source_refs: ["Vandermeulen et al. IGF-1 in feline acromegaly, J Feline Med Surg 2019"]
```
> "Values >1000 ng/mL strongly suggest acromegaly in the appropriate clinical context."

A threshold, attributed to a specific paper, stamped as verified by a system
that cannot verify. `verified` has to mean a person checked it, or it means
nothing, and the whole project rests on that word meaning something.

## Promoting a note back

Per note, in this order:

1. **Read it.** Does it match what you were actually taught?
2. **Check every `source_ref`.** Does the paper exist, and does it say this?
   A citation that cannot be resolved is removed, not kept "just in case".
3. **Check every number.** Doses, thresholds, percentages, reference ranges.
4. Set `provenance:` to what you confirmed — `textbook`, `primary-literature`,
   or `ai-drafted` if it stays a model's draft that you have merely read.
5. Set `confidence:` honestly. `verified` only if you did steps 2 and 3.
   The linter rejects `ai-drafted` + `verified` outright.
6. Set `authored:` to today. You are authoring it now.
7. `git mv` it into `corpus/notes/` and run `make lint`.

A note that survives that is worth more than the fifty that do not. Expect most
of these to be faster to rewrite from your own notes than to audit.

## The gold set

`../../eval/quarantine/gold_generated.jsonl` holds 100 questions generated from
these notes. They cannot be used even if the notes are promoted, because
questions written from a document measure whether retrieval can find the
document they were written from. Recall would look excellent and mean nothing.

Gold questions have to come from questions you actually had while studying.
