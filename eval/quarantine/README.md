# Quarantined gold questions

`gold_generated.jsonl` — 100 questions generated alongside the quarantined
notes in commit `1e8df26`. Not loaded by anything.

**These are circular and cannot be repaired by fixing the notes.** A question
written from a document, evaluated by asking whether retrieval finds that
document, measures nothing. Recall approaches 1.0 by construction. This is the
"gold set flatters the retriever" risk named in the blueprint's risk register.

Kept as a worked example of the failure, and because the *question topics* may
jog your memory when writing real ones. The labels are not reusable.

Real gold questions come from questions you actually had while studying, written
before you look at which note answers them.
